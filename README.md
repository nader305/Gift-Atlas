# 🎁 Gift Atlas Dashboard

A comprehensive, high-end Business Intelligence dashboard built to analyze gift sales performance, customer behavior, and regional distribution patterns. This project transforms raw data into actionable insights with a sleek, user-friendly dark-mode interface.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview
The **Gift Atlas Dashboard** is designed to provide stakeholders with a clear, interactive overview of business performance. It tracks vital metrics such as revenue growth, order volumes, geographical performance, and customer segmentation, allowing data-driven decision-making in the retail/gift industry.

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Metrics
* **Total Sales / Revenue:** Tracked overall financial performance (**$3,520,984**).
* **Total Quantity & Orders:** Monitored total items sold (**3,045**) across total completed orders (**1,000**).
* **Customer Value:** Measured average customer spending behavior (**$6** per transaction).
* **Demographics:** Analyzed buyer distribution by gender to fine-tune future marketing campaigns.

---

## 🚀 Key Visualizations & Features
1. **Sales By Month (Trend Analysis):** Visualizes seasonal purchasing spikes and monthly performance throughout the year.
2. **Top 5 Cities By Sales:** Identifies top-performing regions to optimize inventory and logistics.
3. **Category & Occasions Breakdown:** Breaks down sales by product categories and specific gift occasions (e.g., Anniversary, Birthday, Festivals).
4. **Gender Distribution (%):** A clean donut chart displaying customer gender insights.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
* **Power BI / BI Tool:** For data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive reporting.
* **UI/UX Design:** Dark-mode aesthetic tailored for clean data storytelling and modern dashboard standards.
* **Data Visualization:** Line charts, bar charts, donut charts, and custom KPI cards.
